// About Me

I am a scientist and project team leader specializing in stem cell-based disease modelling, cellular models, and drug discovery. With a PhD in Cell Biology and over 10 years of cross-sector experience spanning academia, pharma, and biotech, my expertise centers on advanced microscopy, quantitative imaging, and target deconvolution.

Head of the Imaging Center at the IGBMC, leading photonic microscopy, image analysis, and assay development. Strong track record in project management, international consortia, target deconvolution, and strategic partnerships with industry, academia, and funding bodies.

// Currently Ongoing Projects

  • Advanced Assay Development: Building high-content 3D stem cell disease models for phenotypic screening and target validation.
  • AI-Driven Image Analysis: Integrating deep learning workflows into core microscopy pipelines for automated target profiling.
  • Consortium Leadership: Directing cross-sector academic and industrial partnerships focused on translational disease models.
